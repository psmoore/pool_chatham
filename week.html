The pool week

Pick a pool from the menu

Everything in one pool, Monday to Saturday: lap swim, swim team practice, lessons, classes and family swim. It opens on the Lap Pool; switch to the Recreation Pool or All.

The pools

Lap Pool50 meters, 7–9 feet deep, 78–82°F. Set up short course (25 yards) all fall. Lap swim, swim teams, deep water aerobics.
Recreation Pool2–5 feet deep, 87–89°F, zero-depth entry. Adult lap swim, family open swim (wristbands), lessons and warm-water classes.
