What does “CLOSED” mean?

“When can I get a lane to myself?”

The Hours page says to swim during a “SLOW” time, and lists the Lap Pool as CLOSED 4–5:30pm with no reason. The reason is on another site: that's when Low Country Aquatic Club's age-group swimmers practice. The same club's schedule also puts swimmers in the pool early on Tuesday and Thursday mornings, at 2pm Monday to Thursday and three evenings a week, while the Hours page shows the pool open for laps.

Here the club practices sit beside the lap lanes on the same calendar, so a swimmer can see the busy times, and the quiet ones, before driving over. When a club changes its season, the staff change it once in an AI chat, and every page shows it.

Questions for the aquatics team

Everything on these calendars comes from what the center and its swim clubs publish. These are the places where two pages disagree, where a time isn't published, or where we had to make a call.

  • GapWhen are the “SLOW” times?
    The Hours page asks swimmers who can't share a lane to “plan your swim during a SLOW time”, but no page says when those are. The club schedules suggest an answer: Low Country Aquatic Club is in the Lap Pool 6–7:30am Tuesday and Thursday, 2–3:30pm Monday to Thursday (homeschool groups) and 5:30–7pm Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, all while the pool is listed open for lap swim. How many lanes do they take?
  • ConflictEvening open swim: 6:30 or 6:45?
    The home page says open swim for families is 12–3:45pm and 6:30–8pm on weekdays. The Hours page reopens the Recreation Pool at 6:45pm. The calendar follows the Hours page.
  • GapSavannah Swim Team, GCAT and Savannah Masters
    Three more clubs train here. The Savannah Swim Team's practice page is still the September 2024 schedule, GCAT lists its Savannah groups without times, and Savannah Masters links to a Facebook group. Only Low Country Aquatic Club and the center's own Chatham Kraken publish current times.
  • GapSaturday's asterisk, and Sundays
    Saturday Lap Pool lap swim 12–6pm carries an asterisk the page never explains. Sunday isn't listed for either pool; the calendar treats the center as closed.
  • GapWater aerobics before October
    The Water Aerobics page shows the October 2026 grid only, so September classes aren't on the calendar. Deep Water classes are in the Lap Pool; everything else is in the warm Recreation Pool.
  • GapWhere is water polo?
    The home page announces Saturday pickup games (youth 1–2pm, adults 2:30–4pm) without saying which pool. We put them in the Lap Pool.
  • ConflictHow many short-course lanes?
    Low Country Aquatic Club describes the Lap Pool as 8 lanes long course or 24 lanes short course, and the Recreation Pool as 6 lanes of 50 yards. The center's own history calls it a 25-yard recreational pool, and pool directories list 20 short-course lanes.
  • OursWhich lanes each program uses
    No page says which lanes lap swim keeps while clubs, lessons and classes are in the water, so everything is booked on its pool and the calendar shows one row per program. Lessons are placed in the Recreation Pool, which the center calls ideal for them; registration doesn't say. In October, Monday and Wednesday Jr. Stroke Stars (6:45–7:15pm) overlaps Adult Stroke Stars (7–7:30pm) and is left flagged.
