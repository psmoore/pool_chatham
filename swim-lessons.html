Group Swim Lessons

Monthly sessions · registration opens the 3rd Wednesday at noon

StarBabies and SuperBabies, StarTots and SuperTots, group lessons for ages 3–10, 11–14 and 15 and older, and the Stroke Stars classes. Classes that start at the same time are one block; use the Practice Groups menu to show one level.

Live from Pool Relay · open the full calendar →

Group classes in a list of 184

Registration lists every class as its own card, and most of the 184 are private lessons: one instructor, one time slot each. The group classes are mixed in, a September and an October copy of each.

On a week, a parent sees that Monday to Thursday a class starts every 45 minutes from 4 to 7pm, and which levels start together.
