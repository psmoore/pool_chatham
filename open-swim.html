Open Swim for Families

Recreation Pool · wristbands required

Family swim in the warm, shallow Recreation Pool, weekday afternoons and evenings and Saturday afternoons, plus Saturday water polo pickup games. Swimmers 15 and younger need someone 18 or older with them.
